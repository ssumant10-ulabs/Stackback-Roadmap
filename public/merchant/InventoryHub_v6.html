Out-of-stock handling

What StackBack does when a subscription delivery is due and an item is out of stock.
Hold until stock is back Active
The delivery is not created while the item is out of stock. It waits in Holds and is created once you update inventory. StackBack never oversells.
This policy applies only to stores that do not continue selling when a variant hits zero. Your store is set to stop at 0, so Hold is active. If you enable "continue selling when out of stock" in Shopify, orders are always created and nothing lands in Holds.
i
Prepaid deliveries roll the schedule forward. Because the customer already paid, a held prepaid delivery is created when stock returns and every later delivery shifts out by the same gap.
