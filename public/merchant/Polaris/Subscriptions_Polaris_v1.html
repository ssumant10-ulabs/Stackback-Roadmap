What this screen is, and is not. Every row is a contract, so everything here is a whole-agreement fact and a whole-agreement action. One delivery inside a contract is an order: it is rescheduled, pushed or retried in Orders, and chased for payment in Payment Reminders. The order timeline is readable from any row here, but the actions on it are not repeated, because exactly one screen owns each action.
Two boundaries worth naming. "Reschedule all deliveries" shifts the whole schedule forward; rescheduling a single shipment is an Orders action. And a contract paused for non-payment reads simply as Paused here, because the reason does not change what you do with the contract. Recovering it is Payment Reminders work, where the contact history and the payment state both live. Filter by Pay per delivery if you want to see which paused contracts sit on the payment side.
