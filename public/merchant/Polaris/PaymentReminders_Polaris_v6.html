Nothing on this tab is overdue yet, which is why there is no overdue column. You send 6 reminders and pause a subscription if it is still unpaid 2 days before its delivery date, both set in Settings, Payments. Until that deadline the invoice is simply due, so the useful number is how long is left, counting down. A payment only becomes genuinely overdue once the subscription has stopped, and those customers are on the Paused tab.
StackBack sends the sequence itself. The pips count those automatic reminders only, because they are what drive the pause. A reminder you send by hand goes out on top of the sequence, does not consume the count, and is shown separately so the row never understates what the customer actually received.
These are the churn risk, and most of them do not know they stopped. The subscription paused itself when the reminder sequence ran out, so the customer took no action and got no decision point. The sequence is finished here, but manual outreach is not capped, so both a call and a reminder are still available on every row.
