Branding

Six colours and three shape controls. Every widget, landing page and section reads them, so a widget you add next month is already themed. Borders, tints and the colour of text on a coloured surface are worked out from these, never set by hand.

Start from your website Recommended

We read your homepage and take the colours from real elements: the canvas behind your cards, your Add to Cart button, your sale badge and your body copy. You can change any of them afterwards.

Selling

Eighteen settings that are store policy rather than per-placement taste, grouped by what a merchant decides first. Hover a row to see what it changes. A template that cannot honour a setting does not draw it, and the stored value is left alone.
