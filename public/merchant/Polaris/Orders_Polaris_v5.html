Two things are absent by design. A pay-per-delivery order whose invoice is unpaid is not here, because the warehouse only prepares what has been paid for; it sits in Payment Reminders until payment clears. A paused subscription's delivery is not here either, because pausing rolls the schedule forward, so there is nothing to pick. Both are Customer Success work.
One boundary worth naming. "Pause deliveries" here pauses the individual delivery, it does not pause the customer's subscription. Pausing a contract is a Customer Success action and lives with the contract, per the rule that exactly one screen owns an action. If Operations needs to stop a whole subscription rather than one shipment, that hands off to CS rather than happening here.
