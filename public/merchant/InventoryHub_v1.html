Out-of-stock handling

What StackBack does when a subscription delivery is due and an item does not have enough stock.
Hold until back in stock Default
Do not create the order. The delivery waits, and ships the moment you restock. Never oversells. Best for physical D2C stock.
Ship what is available
For multi-item deliveries and bundles: ship the in-stock lines now, hold only the short lines.
Ship anyway
Create the order regardless of stock (oversell) and let fulfilment sort it. For backorder or make-to-order merchants.
i
Prepaid deliveries roll the schedule forward. Because the customer already paid, a held prepaid delivery ships when stock returns and every later delivery shifts out by the same gap, so they still receive every delivery they paid for.

v1 scope (this screen): the unified Inventory hub (Forecast + Holds + Settings) chosen over scattering surfaces across Settings / Subscriptions / Dashboard. Policy default = Hold until back in stock; prepaid held deliveries roll the schedule forward; stuck holds are merchant-gated (one-click "Offer customer options"). Status uses a plain colour dot, no glyph (house rule).
Routes into this page: left-nav Inventory (lands on Forecast) · Dashboard "Upcoming 7d" card → Forecast · Notifications: shortfall → Forecast, on hold → Holds, stuck >7d → that row in Holds with "Offer customer options" primed · Subscription detail (held delivery) → Holds · main Settings module keeps a shortcut to Inventory > Settings (configured only here).
DEV must confirm before build: (1) the delivery job checks Shopify variant inventory at run time; (2) an inventory_levels/update webhook can trigger the held-delivery re-check, with an hourly BullMQ backstop; (3) "roll schedule forward" on a prepaid contract is a supported schedule mutation; (4) store-credit issuance path exists for the "credit" choice.
